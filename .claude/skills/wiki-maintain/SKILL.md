# Wiki maintenance

Use this skill when the problem is with the wiki itself rather than a single newly changed source.

## Execution contract

Follow `processes/maintenance.md`.

## Constraints

- Make the smallest change that resolves the demonstrated problem.
- Do not redesign the ontology for neatness alone.
- Do not add taxonomy unsupported by recurring wiki content.
- Preserve provenance and real disagreement through refactors.
