# Wiki ingest

Use this skill for new or materially changed source material after bootstrap.

## Execution contract

Follow `processes/ingest.md` as the end-to-end workflow.

## Central question

What does this source change about what the wiki currently knows?

## Constraints

- Update existing pages before creating new ones.
- Preserve disagreement, uncertainty, and provenance.
- Do not change the ontology for a one-off awkward source.
- Do not leave the wiki knowingly inconsistent after an ontology migration.
